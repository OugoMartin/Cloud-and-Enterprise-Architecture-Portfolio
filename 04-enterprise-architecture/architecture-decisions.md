# Enterprise Architecture Decisions

## Decision 1 — Layered Architecture
Organize the enterprise model into business, application, data, and technology layers so relationships between organizational needs and enabling technology can be communicated clearly.

## Decision 2 — Business-Specific Applications
Keep applications associated with distinct operational needs separated rather than forcing every business unit into a single application model.

## Decision 3 — Shared Infrastructure
Use common infrastructure where appropriate to support efficiency across business units.

## Decision 4 — Centralized Data Management
Maintain centralized data management as a shared architecture capability in the prototype.

## Rationale
The source portfolio identifies the primary challenge as supporting different industry requirements. The selected approach balances separation of business-specific applications with shared infrastructure and centralized data management.
