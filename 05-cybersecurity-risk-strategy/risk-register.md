## Assessment Method
1. Define likelihood criteria.
2. Define impact criteria.
3. Assess each risk consistently.
4. Plot/classify risks using both dimensions.
5. Prioritize mitigation for the most consequential risks.
6. Record rationale and reassess as conditions change.
